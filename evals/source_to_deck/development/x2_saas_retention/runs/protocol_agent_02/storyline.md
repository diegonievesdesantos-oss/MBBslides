# 2026 growth investment: fix retention before adding sales capacity

**Governing thought:** Growth slowed from 25% to 9.2% because we lost customers, not deals. Higher churn explains 80.4% of the €5.6M fall in net new ARR, and most of it comes from first-year customers after the 2025 onboarding cut. Fund retention first: restore onboarding (€0.8M) and hire a gated first wave of 10 AEs, for an estimated €2.0M a year instead of €3.6M. The CRO's 30 AEs would yield at most an estimated €4.5M in 2026, not the €9M promised.

**Key line**
1. **Problem:** Growth slowed because churn more than doubled, from €3.3M to €7.8M, and explains 80.4% of the €5.6M fall in net new ARR (€10.4M to €4.8M). Expansion also fell €1.3M. New-logo ARR rose €0.2M, pipeline created grew 18.0%, deals won rose from 175 to 193 and the win rate held at 23% to 24%. Sales did not break.
2. **Cause:** The leak is in the first year. Customers onboarded in the previous 12 months churned 26% in 2025 against 12% in 2024, and they account for 60.3% of churned ARR (mature customers churned 9%). This followed the February 2025 onboarding cut, which saved €0.8M. Median time to first value went from 21 to 48 days, and 58% of churned customers say they never fully implemented the product. Only 31% cite a competitor or price. Churn above the 2024 rate cost about €2.5M of ARR.
3. **Risk in the current plan:** The 30-AE plan does not hold. The €9M figure assumes 30 AEs at the full €300k for all of 2026. New AEs take 9 months to get there, so Q1 hires deliver at most an estimated €4.5M in 2026, for €3.6M a year. The plan also adds new customers that the current onboarding team cannot retain.
4. **Solution:** A retention-first plan costs an estimated €2.0M a year (€0.8M for onboarding plus €1.2M for 10 AEs) and frees €1.6M. It identifies up to €5.3M of the €5.6M needed to get back to 2024 net new ARR: about €2.5M from first-year churn, about €1.3M from expansion and about €1.5M from AE wave 1. All three are upper bounds, and €0.3M remains an open gap.

**Deck outline** (9 slides)
1. 2026 growth investment: fix retention before adding sales capacity
2. Churn, not sales, cut net new ARR by €5.6M; we recommend a retention-first plan at an estimated €2.0M a year instead of €3.6M for 30 AEs
3. Higher churn explains 80.4% of the €5.6M fall in net new ARR; new-logo ARR still grew €0.2M
4. The sales engine did not break: pipeline grew 18.0%, deals won rose from 175 to 193 and only 31% of churned customers cite a competitor or price
5. First-year customers churned 26% vs 12% in 2024 and account for 60.3% of churned ARR; mature customers churned 9%
6. Cutting onboarding saved €0.8M but more than doubled time to first value (21 to 48 days) and cost about €2.5M of ARR
7. 30 AEs hired in Q1 deliver at most an estimated €4.5M in 2026, not €9M, because new AEs need 9 months to reach €300k (assumes a linear ramp)
8. Retention first costs an estimated €2.0M vs €3.6M and identifies up to €5.3M of the €5.6M needed, leaving an estimated €0.3M gap (all levers are upper bounds)
9. Decisions today: reject the 30 AEs; approve €0.8M for onboarding and a first wave of 10 AEs (our sizing); hold an estimated €1.6M until first-year churn is back at 12%

**Recommendation and next steps:**
- **Reject** the 30-AE plan as proposed (€3.6M a year; the €9M of 2026 new ARR cannot be reached with a 9-month ramp). **Approve** €0.8M a year to bring the onboarding team back to its pre-cut size from Q1 2026 (owner: CEO / VP Customer Success).
- **Approve** a first wave of 10 AEs (€1.2M a year) in Q1 2026 (owner: CRO). **Reallocate** the remaining €1.6M of the request to a reserve for AE wave 2 (owner: CFO).
- **Gate G1, June 2026 board:** release AE wave 2 only if median time to first value is back to 21 days or less, annualised first-year churn of the 2026 cohort is 12% or less, and wave-1 AEs are on the assumed ramp. **Gate G2, end of Q2 2026:** confirm that the expansion lever is on track toward the 2024 level (€4.3M).
- **KPIs:** first-year churn (monthly, target 12% or less), time to first value (monthly, 21 days or less), ARR bridge split into new, expansion and churn (quarterly, back to €10.4M net new), and new ARR per AE against the ramp (monthly). The 2026 target is our own inference (no target appears in the sources) and should be confirmed by the board.
