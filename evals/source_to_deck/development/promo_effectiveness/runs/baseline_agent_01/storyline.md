# Our promotions are buying volume at the cost of margin: the 2026 plan should cut and refocus them, not grow them

**Governing thought:** Our promotions are not creating value: in FY2025 they bought only 2.1% more volume while gross margin fell 2.3 points, and in Snacks and Household every promoted euro loses money. The 2026 plan should reject the proposed 20% budget increase, pull back deep discounts in Snacks and Household, and keep promotions only where they pay back (Fresh and Beverages).

**Key line**
1. Promotions have grown fast but bought little: promo share rose from 29% to 38% of sales and depth from 18% to 24%, yet total volume grew only 2.1% as full-price volume fell 6% and gross margin fell from 27.4% to 25.1%.
2. Most promoted volume would have been sold anyway: 71% is bought by customers who also buy the same item at full price, and promoted items' sales fall 22% in the two weeks after a promotion ends.
3. Value is created in only two of four categories: incremental margin per 100 EUR of promoted sales is +1.6 EUR in Fresh and +0.8 EUR in Beverages, but -2.1 EUR in Snacks and -3.4 EUR in Household.
4. The marketing proposal would amplify the loss: a 20% larger budget and deeper discounts in Household push more money into the category that destroys the most margin per promoted euro.

**Deck outline**
1. Our promotions are buying volume at the cost of margin; we recommend cutting and refocusing them in 2026, not growing them
2. Promotions now drive 38% of sales (up from 29%) at an average depth of 24% (up from 18%)
3. Yet total volume grew only 2.1%, as 14% promoted growth was offset by a 6% fall in full-price volume
4. Gross margin fell 2.3 points, from 27.4% to 25.1%, over the same period
5. Most promoted volume is subsidised: 71% goes to customers who also buy at full price, and sales drop 22% after each promotion
6. Only Fresh (+1.6 EUR) and Beverages (+0.8 EUR) earn incremental margin per 100 EUR promoted; Snacks (-2.1 EUR) and Household (-3.4 EUR) lose it
7. The proposed +20% budget and deeper Household discounts would put more money into the least profitable promotions
8. 2026 plan: shrink and reallocate promotions toward categories and mechanics that pay back
9. Decision requested and next steps

**Recommendation and next steps:**
- Do not approve the proposed 20% increase in the promotion budget or the extension of deep discounts to Household.
- Cut back promotions in Household (-3.4 EUR) and Snacks (-2.1 EUR per 100 EUR promoted), and steer overall promo share and depth back towards FY2024 levels (29% of sales, 18% depth).
- Keep and selectively reinvest in promotions in Fresh and Beverages, the only categories with positive incremental margin.
- Commercial finance and marketing to make incremental margin per promotion the approval criterion for every 2026 event and report it to the board quarterly, alongside gross margin.
