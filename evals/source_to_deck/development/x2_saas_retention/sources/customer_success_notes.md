# Customer success — notes for the planning cycle

- The onboarding team was reduced from 14 to 6 people in February 2025 (saving 0.8 EUR M per year).
- Median time to first value rose from 21 days in 2024 to 48 days in 2025.
- In 2024, first-year customers churned 12%.
- Exit interviews 2025 (64 churned customers): 58% say they never fully implemented the product, 17% moved to a competitor, 14% cite price.
- New account executives reach full productivity (300 EUR k of new ARR per year) after 9 months.
- The support ticket backlog was cleared in November.
