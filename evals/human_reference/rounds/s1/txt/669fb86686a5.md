# Margin recovery 2027: fix the electronics mix to win back gross margin

**Governing thought:** Gross margin fell 1.9 pp because low-margin electronics grew to 19.9% of sales, not because of volume or freight; approve a 2027 plan led by electronics repricing that recovers about 1.4 pp without touching store labour, and make electronics growth margin-conditional, since the budgeted +25% would cost a further 0.7 pp.

**Key line**
1. The 1.9 pp decline is a mix problem: electronics, at 11.5% margin, grew from 13.2% to 19.9% of sales; volume grew and freight sits below gross margin
2. Repricing electronics, restoring grocery and apparel rates and recovering fresh and apparel sales add up to about 1.4 pp, with no store-labour cut
3. The plan only holds if electronics growth becomes margin-conditional: the budgeted +25% would take away a further 0.7 pp

**Deck outline**
1. Electronics mix, not volume or freight, cut gross margin by 1.9 pp; a pricing-led plan recovers about 1.4 pp if electronics growth becomes margin-conditional
2. Revenue grew 7.4% while gross margin fell 1.9 pp, leaving gross profit flat at €385M: this is not a volume problem
3. 1.5 of the 1.9 pp decline is mix: electronics, at 11.5% margin, rose from 13.2% to 19.9% of sales; rate erosion explains the other 0.4 pp
4. Freight rose €9M (+23.7%) after the new carrier contract, but it sits in operating costs: an EBIT lever, not a gross-margin one
5. Electronics is priced 6% below the main competitor; closing the gap at flat volume adds €17.4M gross profit, about +0.9 pp
6. Three levers recover about 1.4 pp of the 1.5 pp target by 2027 — repricing 0.9, category rates 0.3, fresh and apparel recovery 0.2 — with no store-labour cut
7. The 2026 budget's +25% electronics growth would cut a further 0.7 pp at today's 11.5% margin: growth must be conditional on margin
8. Approve the plan with a Q1 2027 electronics price test as the gate, margin-conditional electronics growth and freight renegotiation in the EBIT plan
9. Category P&L: electronics is the only category growing fast, and at the lowest margin
10. Lever sizing rests on four assumptions to validate, led by flat electronics volume after repricing

**Recommendation and next steps:**
- Approve the 2027 gross-margin plan (≈1.4 pp identified vs the 1.5 pp target): electronics repricing towards the main competitor (≈0.9 pp), restoring grocery and apparel margin rates (≈0.3 pp), recovering fresh and apparel sales (≈0.2 pp) — no store-labour cuts.
- Gate the repricing on a Q1 2027 price test on the top 200 electronics references, since volume response is untested.
- Replace the budgeted +25% electronics growth with margin-conditional growth targets (the budget as set would cost a further 0.7 pp).
- Keep freight renegotiation (+€9M since the March 2025 contract) in the EBIT plan and have Finance confirm its classification below gross margin; close the remaining ≈0.1 pp gap with further levers.
