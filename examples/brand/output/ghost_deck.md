# Ghost deck — Repositioning Alvora for profitable growth

**Governing thought:** Alvora should reposition towards discount and fix category margins now to return EBITDA above €1bn by 2028

 2. Alvora can restore EBITDA to €1,067M by 2028 if it repositions towards discount now

### K1 · SITUATION — The Iberian grocery market keeps growing, but growth has moved to discount and online
 3. Discount and online captured 75% of Iberian grocery growth since 2021

### K2 · COMPLICATION — Alvora is losing momentum and margin because its portfolio is tilted to slow segments
 4. Alvora's growth slowed to 3.0% in 2025, below the market (4.1%) for the second year
 5. Alvora holds 14% of supermarkets but only 5% of discount, the segment driving growth
 6. Margin erosion in grocery and convenience (€119M) pushed EBITDA down to €857M
 7. The erosion concentrates in fresh and food-to-go, above all in Madrid and Catalonia
 8. Alvora is stuck in the middle: priced above discounters without a clear experience edge

### K3 · RESOLUTION — A discount banner, a pricing reset and a cost programme restore EBITDA above €1bn by 2028
 9. The target model adds a discount banner and central pricing to fund the growth moves
10. A plan in three waves over 24 months secures quick margin wins before scaling the banner
11. Five initiatives adding €250M lift EBITDA to €1,067M by 2028, net of €40M market headwinds
12. The board is asked to approve three decisions to start Wave 1 in January
