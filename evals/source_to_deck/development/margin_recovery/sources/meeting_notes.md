# Margin review — meeting notes (internal)

Attendees: CFO, Sales director, Category directors, Supply chain lead.

- Sales director: the margin problem is volume. Stores are quieter and we simply sold less this year.
- Category director Electronics: the electronics push worked; electronics is now our fastest-growing category and the 2026 budget assumes another 25% growth.
- Supply chain lead: freight rates rose 24% after the new carrier contract signed in March 2025.
- CFO: the board wants a plan that recovers 1.5 pp of gross margin by 2027 without cutting store labour.
- The FY2025E forecast presented in January showed revenue of €1,500M; the actual close came in lower.
- Pricing team: electronics prices are 6% below the main competitor on the top 200 references.
