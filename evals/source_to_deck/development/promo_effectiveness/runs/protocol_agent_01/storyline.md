# Promotions are destroying value: a smaller, category-targeted 2026 promotion plan

**Governing thought:** Our promotions are destroying value — they shift existing demand rather than create it while gross margin fell from 27.4% to 25.1% — so the board should reject the 20% budget increase and refocus the 2026 plan on Fresh and Beverages, cutting deep discounts in Household and Snacks.

**Key line**
1. Promotions escalated sharply in FY2025: 38% of sales (from 29%) at 24% average depth (from 18%).
2. They shifted rather than created volume, while gross margin fell from 27.4% to 25.1%.
3. Returns split by category: Household and Snacks destroy margin, Fresh and Beverages create it — and the marketing proposal scales the worst of them.
4. 2026 plan: no budget increase, cut depth in Household and Snacks, keep targeted promotion in Fresh and Beverages, and verify with a margin bridge and controlled tests.

**Deck outline** (one line per slide, headline only — max as many slides as the brief's max_slides)
1. Promotions are destroying value: reject the 20% budget increase and refocus 2026 on Fresh and Beverages
2. Promotions rose from 29% to 38% of sales in FY2025, at an average depth up from 18% to 24%
3. Promoted volume grew 14% but full-price volume fell 6%, so total volume grew only 2.1%
4. 71% of promoted volume goes to customers who also buy at full price, and their sales drop 22% in the two weeks after a promotion ends
5. Gross margin fell 2.3 pp, from 27.4% to 25.1%, as promotional depth rose 6 pp
6. Per hundred euros of promotion, Household (−3.4 EUR) and Snacks (−2.1 EUR) lose margin while Fresh (+1.6 EUR) and Beverages (+0.8 EUR) gain
7. Marketing's plan would raise the promotion budget 20% and deepen discounts in Household, the category with the worst return
8. 2026 plan: hold the budget, cut depth in Household and Snacks, target Fresh and Beverages, and confirm with a margin bridge and tests

**Recommendation and next steps:**
- Reject the marketing proposal as submitted: no 20% budget increase and no deep discounts in Household; hold the 2026 promotion budget at the FY2025 level.
- Cut promotional depth and frequency in Household and Snacks, the two categories with negative incremental margin.
- Keep targeted, tested promotions in Fresh and Beverages, the only categories with positive incremental margin.
- Before final budget sign-off, commission a gross-margin bridge (to separate promotional from cost and mix effects) and run controlled promotion tests to confirm incrementality.
