# Packaging supplier consolidation: reject the 100% move to Supplier B, approve a 70/30 split

**Governing thought:** Reject the 100% move to Supplier B and approve a 70/30 split instead. The full move's real saving is €2.87M a year, not €3.0M, and if Supplier B's two stoppages repeat (€4.0M) they would wipe out the €2.93M it adds over the split. The split saves €1.79M a year, nets €4.88M over 2026-2028 and keeps a second plant.

**Key line**
1. Procurement's €3.0M a year is overstated. The 12% cut cannot apply to the €1.1M of rush freight, so the full move saves €2.87M a year gross, or €7.80M over 2026-2028 after €0.8M of switching cost.
2. Relying on Supplier B's one plant is the bigger problem. It stopped twice in three years (9 and 14 days). With all volume on Supplier B, a repeat would cost €4.0M of gross margin. That is more than the €2.93M the full move adds over a 70/30 split, and the incumbent can cover only up to 60% of volume, at two weeks' notice.
3. A 70/30 split (Supplier B at 9% on 70%, the incumbent at 4% on 30%) saves €1.79M a year. After €0.5M of switching cost it nets €4.88M over 2026-2028. The full move nets €3.80M once a repeat of the stoppages is counted.
4. Closing the remaining €1.21M gap to €3.0M is up to us, not the suppliers. €0.65M of the €1.1M rush freight (59%) falls in months the invoices flag as our own forecast errors. Treat this as an upper bound until it has been sized.

7. Today: reject the full move, approve the 70/30 split and a €0.5M switching budget, and commission the rush-freight review

**Recommendation and next steps:**
- Reject procurement's proposal to move 100% of packaging to Supplier B (COO, CFO, CPO). Keeping it would cost €1.07M over 2026-2028 compared with the split if the stoppages repeat, and would leave a single-plant exposure with no cover.
- Approve a 70% Supplier B / 30% incumbent split for 2026-2028 with a €0.5M one-off switching budget. Contract gate (before signature, Q4 2026): both the 9% and 4% offers confirmed in writing, otherwise the decision comes back to the board.
- Continuity gate (Q1 2027): test the incumbent's plan to ramp up and cover Supplier B's share, and review the root causes of Supplier B's force-majeure stoppages. Revisit single sourcing at the 2028 contract review, and only if Supplier B qualifies a second plant.
- Commission a demand-planning review (COO) to size and cut the €1.1M of rush freight. Track realised savings against €1.79M a year (quarterly), rush freight (monthly), stoppage days (monthly) and the volume split (quarterly).
