# Context

**Question:** Should we consolidate packaging on a single supplier, and what is the real saving?
**Decision:** Approve or reject procurement's proposal to move all packaging spend to Supplier B from 2026
**Audience:** COO, CFO, Chief procurement officer · **Max slides:** 8

**What the sources say (neutral summary)**
- *Procurement proposal:* spend €25M in 2025, volume 70% incumbent / 30% Supplier B; Supplier B offers 12% off for 100% of volume with a 3-year commitment, "saving €3.0M a year"; volume breaks: 30% → 0%, 70% → 9%, 100% → 12%.
- *Supply risk review:* Supplier B has one plant and declared two force-majeure stoppages in three years (9 and 14 days); a 9-day stoppage costs €1.6M of gross margin, a 14-day one €2.4M; the incumbent has two plants and can cover up to 60% of volume at two weeks' notice; kept at 30%, the incumbent offers 4% off; switching costs €0.8M (full move) or €0.5M (partial).
- *Invoices 2025 (CSV) and finance note:* the €25M includes €1.1M of rush freight passed through by the incumbent (caused by our forecast errors), to which a price cut does not apply; spend excluding rush freight €23.9M.
